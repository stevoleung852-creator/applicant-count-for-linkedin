See the real applicant count.

LinkedIn hides how many people applied behind “Over 100”. This Chrome extension shows you the exact number on every job.

Free. Works in Chrome on desktop.

How it works

Nothing to set up. Install it and open any job.

1

Open a job

Browse LinkedIn jobs as usual, from search results or a job page.

2

See the number

A small badge in the bottom-left corner shows the exact count, like “148 applicants”.

3

Decide faster

Know your competition before you spend time on an application.
