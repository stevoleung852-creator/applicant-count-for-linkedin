Common questions

Where do I see the count?

Open any job on LinkedIn, either from search results or a job page. A small dark badge appears in the bottom-left corner of the page, for example “148 applicants”.

The badge isn't showing
  1. Make sure you have a job open. The badge only appears when a job is selected.
  2. Reload the LinkedIn tab.
  3. Check the extension is turned on at chrome://extensions.
It says “Applicant count unavailable”

LinkedIn didn't return a count for that job, or asked us to slow down after many lookups in a row. Wait a moment and click the badge to try again.

Why does it say “Over 200 applicants”?

LinkedIn gives exact counts up to 200. Above that, it only shares “Over 200”, so that's what the badge shows.

Why is the number different from what LinkedIn shows?

The extension reads the count from LinkedIn's public job page, which can differ slightly from the figure you see when logged in. LinkedIn also counts people who clicked Apply, not only finished applications.

Does it collect my data?

No. It doesn't collect, store or send any of your data. See the privacy policy.
