Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This policy explains how the Applicant Count for LinkedIn Chrome extension ("the extension", "we", "us") handles your information. The extension is made by Stephen Leung, an independent developer based in Australia. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

The short version

  • The extension doesn't collect, store, sell or share any of your data.
  • There are no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking.
  • It only talks to www.linkedin.com, the site you're already on. It never contacts a server run by us.

What the extension does

When you open a job on www.linkedin.com, the extension reads the job ID from the page address. It then asks LinkedIn for the public version of that same job page, without sending your LinkedIn login, and reads the applicant count from it. It shows that number in a small badge on the page.

What we collect

Nothing. The extension doesn't collect personal information, browsing history, the jobs you look at, or anything else, and nothing is sent to us or to any third party.

Data kept on your device

While a LinkedIn tab is open, the extension remembers the counts it has already looked up, so it doesn't ask LinkedIn twice for the same job. This is kept in memory only and is gone when you close the tab.

Permissions

The extension runs only on www.linkedin.com. It doesn't ask for any other browser permissions, and it can't see other websites you visit.

Children

The extension isn't directed at children under 13, and it doesn't collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
